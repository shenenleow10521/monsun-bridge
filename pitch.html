State × season panel, 50 state-seasons0.00062Not chance

All two-sided. We lead with 0.0083 because three east-coast states make cluster-robust errors anti-conservative; the permutation test needs no such assumption. An association, not a causal estimate.

So fishing-related pathways receive zero allocation: better for workers (SDG 8) and for fish stocks (SDG 14).

  • "Is the fishing drop real? East coast 0.639 against the sheltered west-coast control at 0.977: a −35.4% gap, 95% confidence interval −46.8% to −21.7%."
  • "Our headline p is 0.0083, an exact permutation test: of all 120 ways to label three of ten states as east coast, ours is the most extreme."
  • "We lead with it because with three treated states, clustered standard errors are anti-conservative, so the panel's smaller p, 0.00062, is the weaker evidence. The 0.0025 on the dashboard is the 2023 per-state Mann-Whitney test. The coast-pair test is not significant: five pairs cannot reach it."
  • "It is an association, not a causal estimate. But it is enough to set fishing pathways to zero: good for workers, SDG 8, and for fish stocks, SDG 14."

Next: where machine learning earns its place.

08Where ML earns its place

Machine learning only where it earns its place.

In use, in this build

NLP job matching

TF-IDF → truncated SVD → cosine

Task text from the official MASCO 2020 classification, turned into comparable vectors: 13 documents, 417 terms, 8 components keeping 70.5% of variance (seed 42).

A boat operator matches Marine Operations at 0.96, and every score traces back to task wording.

Multi-criteria ranking

Five weighted criteria, one hard exclusion

Similarity, access, seasonal fit, capacity and training gap, decomposed exactly, not approximated.

Seasonal exclusion: co-seasonal fishing pathways are forced to zero allocation in the model, not in prose.

Time-series nowcast

Ridge and GBR, rolling-origin CV

Chosen on 2018–2022, scored once on held-out 2023 fish landings. One month ahead: 0.731 relative MAE, 27% lower error than seasonal naive (not significant when clustered by state).

Pre-season: 1.050, worse than naive, so not claimed.

Robustness

32 weight combinations

All five scoring weights moved together: 0 of 20 top-ranked pathways change.

The one setting that does matter is published: seasonal fit −25% moves 4 of 20.

Geospatial

Exposure on the map

DOSM district and state boundaries put worker-month exposure and landings retention on the map, so verification starts where exposure is highest.

Automation

Checksum-verified data pipeline

Three datasets downloaded programmatically, each with a SHA-256 checksum and retrieval date (2026-09-14), so the exact bytes can be reproduced. No pre-trained model, no network call at run time.

Proposed roadmap · not built, not evaluated

  1. Learn the ranking weights from pilot placement outcomes instead of analyst settings.
  2. Map free-text job descriptions to MASCO codes with embeddings or an LLM, plus human review during district verification.
  3. Malay-language pathway cards for each worker, generated from the score breakdown.
  4. Use the nowcast as an in-season severity monitor, not a pre-season forecast.
  • "Where does machine learning earn its place? First, NLP job matching: TF-IDF, truncated SVD and cosine similarity over MASCO task text. 13 documents, 417 terms, 8 components keeping 70.5% of the variance. Explainable by construction."
  • "Second, a multi-criteria ranking with a hard seasonal exclusion. Third, a time-series nowcast: ridge and gradient boosting, chosen by rolling-origin cross-validation on 2018 to 2022 and scored once on held-out 2023. One month ahead: 0.731 relative MAE, 27% lower error. Before the season opens it scored 1.050, worse than naive, so we do not claim it."
  • "Plus robustness across 32 weight combinations, a geospatial exposure map, and a checksum-verified pipeline."
  • "And clearly labelled as proposed, not built: learn the weights from pilot placements; map free-text job descriptions to MASCO with embeddings or an LLM plus human review; Malay-language pathway cards; and the nowcast as an in-season severity monitor."

Do not claim the pre-season forecast, and do not present the roadmap items as built.
