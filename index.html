MONSUN BRIDGE

The monsoon is predictable. So is the plan.cost.rollout.

Every November the Northeast Monsoon closes the islands of Malaysia's east coast. Boats stop, jetties shut, and thousands of tourism workers stop earning for four and a half months. Monsun Bridge plans that transition before the window opens, built only from open Malaysian official statistics.

The problem

The shock is dated. And invisible in the national numbers.

1
A national authority dates it in advance

METMalaysia reviews and publishes every Northeast Monsoon window. The 2024/25 season ran 133 days; reviewed seasons run 132 to 138.

2
The national series never flinches

Visitors move inland and west, so national tourism demand holds. The loss is district-level and occupational. It reaches no indicator.

3
Nobody counts the workers

Malaysia publishes no register of tourism workers and no measure of their income loss. We count what can be observed: worker-months of exposure.

Monsoon clock · Northeast Monsoon 2024/25
133days, 13 Nov 2024 → 25 Mar 2025 · Observed
0.93Monsoon Share of Q1 2025 · Derived

Quarterly statistics cannot see a window that opens mid-November. Monsoon Share weights each quarter by the days it spends inside the published window, so a dated event becomes a statistical weight.

“For a boat operator in Besut the monsoon is not a slow season. It is a stop.”A shock that a national authority dates in advance should be the easiest kind to plan for. Nothing plans for it.

The four claims the dashboard defends
Claim 1 · ObservedThe shock is predictable and officially dated.

METMalaysia has reviewed and published the Northeast Monsoon window for each season; 2024/25 ran 133 days against a 132-day climatological mean.

Claim 2 · DerivedThe harm is invisible in national statistics.

Visitors redistribute inland and west, so the disruption is district-level and occupational. It has to be measured in worker-months, not visitor counts.

Claim 3 · DerivedThe obvious answer is wrong, and we tested it.

Routing stranded boat crews into fishing fails: in Terengganu and Pahang every non-monsoon month of 2023 out-landed every monsoon month. Exact U = 0, p = 0.0025.

Claim 4 · ScenarioThe plan is priced, and its break-even is stated.

Coverage costs RM 887 per worker-month. The programme returns what it costs once roughly a third of participants are placed at the published median wage.

Method

Four grades. One weighting device. No hidden assumptions.

118 official records across seven open datasets. Three downloads carry a SHA-256 checksum and a retrieval date, so the exact bytes can be verified. Nothing is simulated, fabricated or synthetic.

Observed

A published official value

The 2024/25 window ran 133 days (METMalaysia).

Derived

A formula or test over observed inputs

Monsoon Share 0.93 for Q1 2025; the −35.4% retention gap.

Modelled

The 13,703-worker cohort and 52,655 worker-months.

Scenario

RM 887 per worker-month. Move the slicer and it moves.

Rollout

Start in Besut. Validate. Expand.

No new agency, no new survey, no new law. Every phase runs on an institution that already exists and already publishes.

Stage 1 · indicative season 2026/27

Besut pilot

100 proposed participants, RM 266,200 indicative budget. Verify workers, employer vacancies and training; record placements, months worked and wages.

Expansion gateAt least 43 of 100 placed, under a rule fixed in advance.
Stage 2 · indicative season 2027/28

Four-district rollout

Besut, Marang, Kuala Nerus and Rompin. Modelled planning cohort 13,703; at 20% participation about 2,741 participants, RM 7.30M.

Expansion gateProceed only after the pilot passes and employers reconfirm seasonal vacancies.
Stage 3 · 2028/29 onward

Wider district adoption

Extend to districts with available official data: DOSM measures the district, METMalaysia dates the window. Recalibrate and evaluate each expansion.

Expansion gateValidate locally before expanding. No national adoption is implied.
