MONSUNBRIDGE

The monsoon is predictable. So is the plan.

Every November the Northeast Monsoon closes the islands of Malaysia's east coast, and tourism work stops for about four months. Monsun Bridge plans that transition before the window opens: who is exposed, which work they can move to, and what it costs to cover them. Built only from open Malaysian official statistics.

05aMethod: comparing jobs

How we compare job tasks

We turn official job descriptions into numbers and measure how similar two jobs are.

  1. 1MASCO task text

    The official description of each job's tasks.

  2. 2TF-IDF

    Turns the text into numbers, weighting words distinctive to a job.

  3. 3Truncated SVD

    Groups related words into a few themes.

  4. 4Cosine score

    Higher scores indicate more similar task descriptions.

Vacancy verification. Rankings are not job offers. Employers must confirm vacancies before participants are enrolled.

Method details

Score = 0.40 × task similarity + 0.20 × geographic access + 0.20 × seasonal fit + 0.10 × sector capacity − 0.10 × (training months ÷ 3).

The training gap is an assumption: months of bridging = max(0.5, 3 × (1 − similarity)), so it is not independent of similarity.

Without seasonal exclusions, 28–41% of the allocation goes to the three co-seasonal pathways across all 20 worker groups.

Could the gap be due to chance?

Four statistical tests, all two-sided

TestWhat it comparespResult
Exact permutationAll 120 ways 3 of 10 states could be labelled east0.0083Not chance
State × season panelEast vs west across 50 state-seasons0.00062Not chance
Per-state comparisonTerengganu and Pahang, monsoon vs other months, 20230.0025Not chance
Coast-pair testFive matched season pairs0.0625Not significant

Three of four tests rule out chance. The fourth has only five pairs, too few to reach significance.

Why we lead with p = 0.0083

Only three states are on the treated east coast. With so few clusters, cluster-robust standard errors are anti-conservative, so the panel's smaller p (0.00062) is the weaker evidence. The exact permutation test makes no such assumption: our east-coast labelling is the most extreme of all 120 possible labellings.

Designs: state × season difference-in-differences with season fixed effects and state-clustered SE (n = 50); exact permutation (n = 120); Wilcoxon signed-rank on coast pairs (n = 5, whose smallest possible two-sided p is 0.0625); Mann-Whitney on 2023 landings (n = 12, U = 0).

Where does RM 887 come from?

Cost per participant at the default settings: one training month and two support months at RM 1,100 a month

RM 1,100 RM 1,320 242
Training1 month × RM 1,100

RM 2,662per participant÷ 3worker-months covered= RM 887per worker-month

Each participant costs RM 2,662 for three months of cover, which is RM 887 per worker-month.

How the default scenario is set

Default settings: 20% participation, 1 training month, 2 support months, RM 1,100 a month, stipend at 60% of the training rate, administration at 10%. 2,741 participants × RM 2,662 = RM 7.30M. Paying the full unit rate on all three months would give RM 9.05M; we show the factor so the headline reconciles.

Placement rate to offset costs = cost per worker-month ÷ median monthly wage of the destination group, which assumes a placed participant earns that wage for three months.

All of these are graded Scenario: move a slicer on dashboard page 04 and every figure on this page moves with it. The four districts employ 210,200 people in total.
